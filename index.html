Tips for buying clothes from Japan

  • Compare with a garment you own. Lay your favorite shirt flat, measure it the same way as the size chart, and compare. This is more reliable than size labels.
  • Watch for "flat" measurements. A 身幅 of 55 cm means a chest circumference of about 110 cm (43.3 in).
  • Letter sizes are not universal. A Japanese M is often closer to a US S. Some brands use numbers (1, 2, 3 or 0, 1, 2) instead of letters — check their chart.
  • Read the fit notes. Words like オーバーサイズ (oversized), ゆったり (relaxed) or タイト (tight) next to the chart tell you how the brand intends it to fit.
  • Check return rules before ordering. International returns can be expensive, so getting the size right the first time matters.
