How it works

  • Safe neutrals — black, white, gray, navy, beige and denim work with almost any color.
  • Tone on tone — colors close to yours on the color wheel, made lighter or darker, give a calm, put-together look.
  • Contrast — the opposite color on the color wheel, toned down so it is easy to wear, makes the outfit stand out.
Color suggestions are for reference only. Colors in photos can look different depending on lighting and your screen.
