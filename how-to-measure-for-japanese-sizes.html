How to measure for Japanese sizes

The most reliable way to pick a Japanese size is not your body measurements or your usual label. It is a garment you already own and like the fit of.

What you need

  • A shirt, jacket or pair of pants that fits you the way you like
  • A tape measure (or a piece of string and a ruler)
  • A flat surface like a table or the floor

Tops: 4 measurements

  • Length (着丈) — lay the top flat, front side up. Measure from the highest point of the shoulder down to the hem.
  • Body width (身幅) — measure straight across, from armpit seam to armpit seam.
  • Shoulder width (肩幅) — measure from one shoulder seam to the other.
  • Sleeve length (袖丈) — measure from the shoulder seam to the end of the cuff.

Pants: 4 measurements

  • Waist (ウエスト) — button the pants, lay them flat and measure straight across the top of the waistband.
  • Rise (股上) — from the crotch seam up to the top of the waistband, on the front.
  • Inseam (股下) — from the crotch seam down the inside of the leg to the hem.
  • Thigh width (渡り幅) — straight across the leg, just below the crotch.

Compare and choose

Write your numbers down in centimeters, then compare them with the size chart on the product page. Pick the size whose numbers are closest to yours, especially body width for tops and waist and inseam for pants.

Remember that flat measurements are half the circumference. Measure your own clothes flat too, so you are comparing like with like.
