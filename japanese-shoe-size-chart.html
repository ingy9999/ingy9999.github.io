Sizes vary by brand. UK and EU equivalents in particular can differ by half to one size between brands (for example, Nike and adidas use different charts). Use this table as a starting point and check the brand's own chart when it has one.

How to find your Japanese shoe size

  • Measure your foot. Stand on a piece of paper, mark your heel and longest toe, and measure the distance in cm. That is roughly your Japanese size.
  • Measure both feet and use the longer one.
  • Check the width. Japanese sizes also use width codes like E, 2E and 3E. If you have wide feet, look for these on the product page.
