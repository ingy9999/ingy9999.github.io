How to read a Japanese size chart

Japanese online stores list the measurements of the garment itself, in centimeters and in Japanese. Here is what each term means.

Why some numbers look too small

Many Japanese stores measure clothes laid flat on a table. A measurement like 身幅 (body width) is taken across the front only, so it is half of the full circumference.

For example, a 身幅 of 55 cm means the chest is about 110 cm (43.3 in) all the way around. The same goes for waist, hip, thigh and hem widths when they are measured flat.

Garment size is not body size

The numbers on a Japanese size chart describe the clothing, not the person wearing it. A shirt with a 110 cm chest is meant to fit someone with a smaller chest, because clothes need room to move. The easiest way to compare is to measure a piece of clothing you already own. See how to measure for Japanese sizes.
