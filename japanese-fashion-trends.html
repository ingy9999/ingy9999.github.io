What's trending in Japanese fashion this week

Updated November 23, 2026 (JST)

A quick, sourced look at what people in Japan were buying, searching for and wearing this past week: jackets with military details, winter character collabs from GU, the big UNIQLO and GU sale week, and a run of sneaker and watch collabs.

1. Napoleon jackets are the jacket of the moment

What: Short jackets with military-style button fronts and stand collars, called “Napoleon jackets” in Japan, are the standout search term among young shoppers on ZOZOTOWN, Japan’s biggest fashion store.

Why: WWD Japan reports that searches by Gen Z users for the style jumped about 155-fold in its October 2026 ranking, and retailers such as Re:EDIT list it among this season’s key items.

Try it from abroad: Look for a cropped jacket with a row of buttons and a high collar, and wear it over a simple knit with straight or flared pants. Japanese jackets are often cut short and narrow in the shoulders, so check the shoulder width (肩幅) before ordering.

Sources: WWD Japan (Nov 20, 2026), Re:EDIT

2. Tailored jackets, flared pants and long necklaces

What: Street snaps collected by TRILL for its November issue put three things on top: tailored jackets, flared pants and long necklaces, with chokers coming back as well.

Why: Tailored jackets are being worn in a softer way, with sleeves pushed up and paired with feminine pieces, while flared pants bring back a Y2K silhouette.

Try it from abroad: A blazer with the sleeves rolled, flared jeans and one long pendant necklace is an easy version to copy. When buying Japanese pants online, compare the inseam (股下) to your own, since many styles run shorter than US sizes.

Sources: TRILL (Nov 1, 2026)

3. Burgundy, cherry red and mocha brown lead the colors

What: Japanese retailers’ autumn/winter 2026 color guides keep pointing to deep reds (burgundy and cherry red) and warm browns such as mocha, alongside olive green and dark gray.

Why: These colors fit the season’s classic and trad mood and pair well with the grays and beiges people already own. Oggi’s November outfit guide also suggests brown with a touch of mint.

Try it from abroad: Start with one piece, such as a burgundy knit or a brown jacket, and keep the rest neutral.

4. GU × Chiikawa winter collab

What: GU’s autumn/winter collab with the character Chiikawa has a snowy-day theme. The first drop of Nordic and jacquard sweaters and sweatshirts came out on November 20, and a second drop of fleecy loungewear follows on November 27.

Why: Prices run from ¥990 to ¥3,990, and the launch fell on the first day of GU’s sale week, so popular pieces have a purchase limit of two per design and color.

Try it from abroad: If you order from Japan through a proxy shopping service, remember that Japanese sizes often run smaller than US sizes, so compare the measurements, not just the letter.

5. UNIQLO and GU “Kanshasai” sale week (Nov 20–26)

What: UNIQLO’s yearly thanks-giving sale runs from November 20 to 26, with lower prices on HEATTECH, fleece and PUFFTECH jackets. GU is running its own sale on the same days, with knit cardigans and faux-shearling boots among the featured deals.

Why: This is Japan’s biggest week for winter basics, and UNIQLO also launched its UNIQLO and Cecilie Bahnsen autumn/winter 2026 collection during the sale.

Try it from abroad: UNIQLO runs its own stores in many countries, but sale prices and dates differ by country, so check your local store before using a proxy service.

6. A busy week for sneaker and watch collabs

What: Mid-November brought a run of limited releases in Japan: ASICS × Kiko Kostadinov’s new NOVALIS shoes (Nov 17), a limited SALOMON XT-6 colorway at KICKS LAB. in Harajuku (Nov 21), and a deep green-dial CASIO × A.P.C. watch (Nov 17).

Why: Several of these were limited to specific stores in Japan, which is why collectors watch release dates closely.

Try it from abroad: Japanese shoe sizes are listed in centimeters of foot length, so convert before you order a limited pair you can’t easily return.

7. Moccasins and slip-ons with sneaker soles

What: Moccasins and deck shoes rebuilt with sneaker-style soles are getting attention in Japan, and CAMPER’s all-black GORE-TEX version of its Peu Serra slip-on is one of the autumn/winter 2026 picks.

Why: They sit between dress shoes and sneakers, and a GORE-TEX version is made for wet winter days.

Try it from abroad: Pair them with cropped or straight pants so the shoe shows. For slip-ons, fit matters more than with laced shoes, so measure your foot in centimeters first.
